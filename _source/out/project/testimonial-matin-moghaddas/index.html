Testimonial

Dr. Matin Moghaddas, D.O.

Hospitalist Physician & Family Medicine Residency Faculty · Co-founder, Maaples

I am pleased to recommend Hamed Nouri for professional opportunities within your organization. I am a Hospitalist Physician and Family Medicine Residency Faculty member with additional experience in clinical research, entrepreneurship, technology commercialization, and university based innovation. I have led NSF funded ventures and collaborated with researchers and startup teams at institutions including Cornell University and New Mexico State University.

I worked closely with Hamed for several years while building Maaples, an AI focused technology venture for education. What impressed me most was his ability to enter unfamiliar areas, learn quickly, and convert that knowledge into useful solutions for the team. A strong example occurred when we entered a business plan competition. Although Hamed had limited prior experience in business development, he independently learned the relevant concepts, helped develop our proposal and commercialization strategy, and contributed to our team receiving a Sandia National Laboratories innovation award and $15,000 prize. That experience reflected a pattern I saw throughout our work together: Hamed is a remarkably fast learner who consistently turns new knowledge into practical improvements that make a real impact on the team.

He also demonstrated strong instincts for identifying and adopting technologies that improved how our team worked. He used emerging platforms to strengthen project management, communication, knowledge organization, and productivity. That same capability is reflected in his broader work across AI assisted workflows, automation, digital operations, knowledge management, technical communication, and product development. He is comfortable working across disciplines, adapting to changing tools and requirements, and finding ways to make systems and processes more effective.

I recommend Hamed without reservation. I believe he would be especially valuable to organizations seeking someone who can quickly understand a new environment, identify opportunities for improvement, and take ownership of turning ideas into practical results. I would gladly work with him again.
