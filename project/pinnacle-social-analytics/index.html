Pinnacle Social Analytics

Google Workspace · Notion · Automation Pipeline

An end-to-end pipeline connecting Google Drive, Google Sheets, and Notion with a series of custom apps that bulk-process client media and keep the asset manager and content calendar updated automatically.

The Problem

Client media moves through several distinct processing steps before it's ready to publish, and tracking what's been processed, where it lives, and what's scheduled next was manual and easy to lose track of.

Asset status and the content calendar were tracked separately from the tools doing the actual processing, so they constantly fell out of sync.

The Solution

I connected Google Drive, Google Sheets, and Notion with a set of custom-built apps that bulk-process media through each step of the pipeline, from raw upload through to finished, branded output.

As media moves through the pipeline, the Google Sheets asset manager and the Notion content calendar update automatically, so status always reflects what's actually been processed.

Highlights

  • Google Drive + Sheets + Notion integration
  • Bulk processing through a chain of custom apps
  • Automatic asset-manager updates
  • Automatic content-calendar updates
  • End-to-end tracking from raw media to publish-ready output
